Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent Elements Milton homes website at https://elementsmiltonhomes.com ("this site") collects, uses, and protects information submitted by visitors. This site is not the builder’s official website.

What we collect

When you submit a registration form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and phone number, plus consent status, the page path you submitted from, and standard marketing attribution data (such as UTM parameters) when those values are present in the URL.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to contact you about Elements Milton homes and similar pre-construction homes that may interest you, according to the consent you give when you submit the form.

How it is stored

Submitted information is stored with Supabase, a third-party data processor, using row-level security controls. Analytics on this site may be processed through Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel, which can set cookies to measure site use and advertising performance.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

You give express consent (in line with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation, CASL) when you check the consent box and submit the form. You may withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any message you receive.

Requesting deletion

To ask that your information be deleted, reply “unsubscribe” to any message you receive or contact us through the details available on this site. We process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe under PIPEDA.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies for Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Compliance

Data practices on this site are intended to follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and CASL.
